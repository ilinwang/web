Research Interests

I develop optimization models and algorithms for network, logistics, transportation, and scheduling problems. Current themes:

  • Network Optimization & Algorithms. Shortest paths (multiple-pairs, algebraic and decomposition methods), maximum flow and minimum-cost flow, multicommodity network flows, network compaction schemes and instance generators, and sensor-network localization.
  • Shared Mobility & Sustainable Transportation. Design, deployment, and (crowdsourced) repositioning strategies for public bike-sharing, e-scooter, and car-sharing systems; recharging strategies for electric-vehicle fleets.
  • Logistics & Supply Chain Management. Reverse / e-waste logistics network design, motorcycle-courier and city-logistics networks, crowdshipping with smart lockers, and first/last-mile delivery.
  • Unmanned Vehicles & Path Planning. Coordinated routing, search, and patrol planning for fleets of UAVs, unmanned surface vehicles, and autonomous ground vehicles; drone light-show and sheep-herding path planning; carrier-vehicle routing and truck platooning.
  • Humanitarian & Disaster Logistics. Multi-mode network, arc, and node restoration scheduling for post-disaster recovery; recovery-team routing and ambulance redeployment.
  • Warehousing & Manufacturing Systems. Robotic movable-fulfillment and shuttle-based storage/retrieval systems, high-density puzzle-based storage picking, and semiconductor / hybrid flow-shop scheduling.
  • Network Interdiction, Security & Resilience. Security deployment and patrol scheduling over rail-transit networks, network interdiction for disrupting illicit trafficking, prescribed-burning strategies against forest fires, and offshore wind-farm cable topology.
  • Social Networks & Other Topics. Influence-maximization scheduling (with “majority illusion”), project scheduling under disruption, pavement rehabilitation planning, haplotype inference / bioinformatics, and data mining.
